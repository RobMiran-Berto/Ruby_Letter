There are some things I should have said better, sooner, and with more honesty.

I'm Sorry, Ruby.

Ruby, I don't even know if these words can fully explain what has been on my heart, but I need you to know that I am truly sorry.

I was wrong. I was wrong about so many things, and I hate that it took losing you and being without you for me to understand some of them. I can look back now and see the moments where I could have listened better, communicated better, understood you better, and simply loved you the way you deserved to be loved.

I don't want to make excuses for myself. I don't want to explain away the things I did wrong. I just want to take responsibility for them. You deserved patience. You deserved understanding. You deserved to feel safe, appreciated, heard, and loved. I'm sorry for every moment when I made you feel otherwise.

The hardest part is that I can't stop thinking about you. No matter how much I try to distract myself, somehow my mind finds its way back to you. I miss your presence. I miss talking to you. I miss the little things that I probably didn't appreciate enough while I had them.

I still look at the letter you made me for Valentine's Day. I look at it every day. I don't just see words on a page when I look at it. I see you. I see the love and time you put into making it for me, and it reminds me of how lucky I was to have someone who cared about me that deeply.

And I still look through our pictures from the zoos. I'll see one and suddenly I'm back there with you, remembering the day, the little moments, the laughs, and what it felt like to have you beside me. Those memories mean more to me now than I can put into words.

I miss you, Ruby. I miss us. And more than anything, I wish I could go back and handle things differently. If I could have another chance, I would want to show you—not just tell you—that I understand where I went wrong and that I am willing to do the work to become better.

I love you. I have loved you, and being apart has made me realize just how deeply that love is rooted in me. I don't expect an apology to erase anything or make everything okay. I know I can't ask you to forget what happened. I can only tell you the truth: I am sorry, I love you, I miss you, and I wish we were together again.

If there is ever a day when you are willing to talk to me again, I would be grateful for the chance to listen—not argue, not defend myself, not try to prove that I was right. Just listen to you.

Whatever happens, I hope you always know that you mattered to me more than I knew how to express. You still do.

— Berto ❤️
